Services

The audits a finance team already knows. Start with one, or with accounts payable.

Accounts payable recovery

Duplicate payments, overpayments, prices above the signed rate, and tax charged in error. Each amount comes back as a documented claim.

Statement reconciliation

Vendor statements checked against what was paid. Unapplied credits and open items come back as claims.

Contract compliance

What was billed, set against the signed price, discount, and rebate. The claim shows the term and the invoice that broke it.

Retail recovery

Invalid deductions, shortages, and chargebacks. The claim names the deduction and the record that shows it was not owed.

Rebates and promotional funding

Volume terms and funding that were agreed and never claimed. The claim ties the amount to the agreement.

Freight

Freight charges that do not match the agreed rate, the route, or the terms.

How a recovery audit runs.

Your team’s time is the scope, the file, and the sign-off. A supplier sees a claim only after you approve it.

  1. 01

    Plan. The audit, the records, and the suppliers to leave out.

  2. 02

    Secure the file. Read-only records, held on our servers.

  3. 03

    Audit. The whole file, not a sample. Each finding names its source.

  4. 04

    Review. You approve the claim before a supplier sees it.

  5. 05

    Recover. No recovery, no fee.
