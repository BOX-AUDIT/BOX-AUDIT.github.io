Services

The audits a finance team buys from a recovery firm. Start with one, or with a scan of accounts payable.

Accounts payable recovery

Duplicate payments, prices above the signed rate, unused credits, and tax charged in error. Each amount comes back as a documented claim.

Contract compliance

What was billed, set against the signed price, discount, and rebate. The claim shows the term and the invoice that broke it.

Rebates and freight

Volume terms and freight charges that were agreed and never claimed. The claim ties the amount to the agreement.

Retail recovery

Invalid deductions and chargebacks. The claim names the deduction and the record that shows it was not owed.

Products

The tools that run those services. They are how the audit is done, not a separate software seat.

Inbox Trace

Reads the email where the deal was agreed. Finds the price, rebate, or funding term, and where it was not applied.

Unbox

Reads invoices, contracts, and price files, and turns them into the records the audit checks.

Box Agent

Builds the documented claim from those records and stops before it is filed. A person signs it.

An advanced stack, built for this audit.

The intelligence was built by people from Microsoft, Intuit, and Amazon. It runs on Box Audit’s own servers, and it reads the whole file, not a sample.

Complete security for the records.

The records are used for the audit they were given for. The bar is the one finance teams apply under SOC 2: security of the system, confidentiality of the records, and integrity of the result.
